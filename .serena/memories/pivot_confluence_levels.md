Resistance levels should be built as clean confluence zones, not every local daily swing. Daily pivots are broad zones; 5-minute HOD pivots can stand alone and, when near a daily/whole-dollar zone, should refine the displayed level to the exact 5-minute HOD pivot price (e.g. FOXX: 5.90 replacing a broad daily/whole-dollar 6 area). It is acceptable if lower daily pivots like ~4 or ~5.09 are not detected, as long as matching 5-minute HOD pivots such as ~5.11 survive. Pure static whole/half/quarter dollar levels should stay available internally but should be hidden from the `-l levels` display unless represented by a real structural/dynamic level.
5-minute session double tops count as structural resistance when at least two distinct local tops are within 2% of that session's HOD; store/display the resistance at the actual session HOD with source `5-min HOD double top`.
